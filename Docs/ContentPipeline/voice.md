# Deadwater Run - Voice Sheet

**Status: draft for Brandon's approval.** Taken from `DeadwaterRun.md` (GDD), the store page drafts
(`Docs/Release/StorePage.md` L2/S3) and `context/`. The content critic scores against this, so change it here
and every later review follows.

## The setting
- The legendary seas of Aquadia. Rival pirate crews, ships with health bars, and the Kraken at the end of the chart
  that "has never been beaten"
- The tone is a small, tough crew against a cruel sea. Dangerous and tense, with dry gallows humour. It isn't a cartoon and it isn't grimdark
- The sea is mostly kind (60% good events) but never safe

## How it sounds
- **Plain and punchy.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns: hull, powder, rope, rum, gold, deck, rigging, fog
- **Second person, present tense.** "A longboat bumps against your hull."
- **Dry, not jokey.** Understatement over punchlines. "That will have to do."
- **Light pirate flavour.** One salty word per line at most. No "arr", "matey", "ye" or "shiver me timbers"
- **Stakes are physical:** gold, HP, the ship, the crew. Permadeath is real, so treat crew lives with weight
- **Choices read as decisions**, not as menu items. "Cut the line" rather than "Option A: lose 20 gold"

## Avoid
- Modern slang, memes, fourth-wall jokes
- Long lore dumps. A body is 2-4 sentences
- Magic systems, gods, curses with rules, anything the game can't show or doesn't have
- Real-world places, people or brands, and names or text from other games
- Promising effects the outcomes don't apply

## Reference lines (from the store page draft)
- "The seas of Aquadia have never been kind, and the Kraken has never been beaten."
- "You have a ship, a captain and two crewmates. That will have to do."
- "Quick and safe, or slow and rich - your call, every fight."
- "Lose a pirate and they're gone for good."
