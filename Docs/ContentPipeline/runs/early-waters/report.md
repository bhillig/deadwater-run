## Final events (best round)

### The Grounded Sloop  `DA_Event_TheGroundedSloop` - 90/100

_This is the batch's best event. Sinking gives 60, plundering gives 90 at a crew cost, and the Fearmongerer surrender lifts its mechanics straight from the GDD. The voice is tight ("ask to keep their buckets"). The only flaw is that "Two shots and she goes under" sinks a ship that is grounded on a reef._

```
Title:    The Grounded Sloop
Valence:  Good      Layers: 1-3        Weight: 1
Body:     A rival sloop sits hard on a reef, heeled over, her crew bailing with buckets. Her cannons point at the sky. You could put two shots into her and fish out what floats, or go over her rail and take the hold whole.

```
