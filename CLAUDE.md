## How I like to work

1. **Plan first.** For anything bigger than a quick fix, show me a short plan before you build.
2. **Prove it works.** Never tell me something is done without proving it actually works.
3. **Do it properly.** If a fix feels hacky, redo it the right way. Finish the whole job - never leave loose threads.
4. **Keep it tidy.** Every project lives in its own folder. Never dump loose files at the root.
